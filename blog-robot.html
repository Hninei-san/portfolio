LEARNING LOG

How I learn stuff I have no clue about

I wanted to build a UAV-UGV robot and had no idea how. This is less about robots and more about how I got unstuck and keep it going.

UAV-UGV combines an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) with an unmanned ground vehicle (UGV), allowing transformation to travel as a drone and mobile robot.

" The motivation for this project came from the 2025 Myanmar earthquake, where collapsed buildings made inspection difficult and dangerous. A ground robot may not be able to cross rubble, while a drone alone has high enery consumption and instability in cluttered environments "

ROUTE MAP

The Learning Path

01

Background research

I didn't know what parts I needed or what to even google first. The blank-page feeling was the worst part. So, I look for the existing similar research. These are the questions I studied:

  • What transformable robots have already been developed?
  • Components
  • Calculations
  • Weight and design considerations
  • Software stacks
02

Define objectives and scope

After spending two months researching and learning new tools without really knowing where the project would go, we decided to define objectives and scope. This helped us understand what we wanted to achieve and stick to the plan.

Design and fabricate the mechanical structure for both ground and aerial operation. Develop the transformation mechanism so the robot can switch between UGV and UAV modes. Develop the control system using RC, flight controller, and ROS 2. Integrate sensors and onboard computing for robot operation and data processing. Add visual inspection to detect objects and obstacles. Create a web interface to view the robot's camera feed and detection results in real time. Test the robot to see how well it moves, transforms, operates, and performs inspection.
03

Reverse Engineering

After defining the scope, I have to do background research again.

  1. 1

    Research + reverse engineer

    • Look up how existing UAV and UGV robots are built
  2. 2

    Learn UGV and UAV

    • Learn how a ground robot moves and steers
    • Learn how a drone flies and stays stable
  3. 3

    Do the calculations

    • Work out weight, Electrical System & Design, power and battery needs
  4. 4

    Prepare materials

    • Turn calculations into component list
  5. 5

    Software stack

    • Pick the software and simulation
04

Planning

Organize the project into three main areas: hardware, electrical, software integration.

Hardware

learn

  • how each componet work
  • wiring + power
  • Customized mechanical design
Electrical

learn

  • PCB design
  • Good wiring practices
Software Integration

learn

  • Software-in-the-Loop (SITL) simulation
  • Firmware
  • Drone control
  • Mobile Robot control
  • Remote control module

This is the place I wish I could start the project earlier. So many problems !!!!!

05

Ta da da !! Final product

After several tests and failures, I finally got the robot working!
